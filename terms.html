What Brickline is

Brickline is a research tool. It gathers public data about places in Los Angeles County: Census figures, county assessor records, hazard maps, zoning and city rules.

What it is not

  • Not an appraisal. Price figures are estimates from public records, not a valuation of any property.
  • Not legal, financial, tax or real estate advice.
  • Not a guarantee of what you can build. "Room to build" is a first screening. Check with the city, a licensed professional and a title report.

Public data can be out of date, incomplete or wrong. Every figure shows its source; confirm anything you rely on.

Using the site

  • Use it for your own research. Don't scrape it, resell it or overload it.
  • The site, its design and its code belong to Brickline. The underlying public data belongs to its publishers.
  • We may change or stop the service at any time, especially during the pilot.

To the extent the law allows, Brickline isn't liable for decisions made using the site. The service is provided "as is".
