Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026. DRAFT: to be reviewed before launch.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you give it to get your results or an alert.
  • Your answers to the questions, and the addresses you look up.
  • A scrambled visitor code made from your internet address, used only to stop abuse. We don't keep the address itself.

What we use it for

  • To email you the results you asked for.
  • To ask, occasionally, what you thought of the tool.
  • To improve Brickline.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it except with the services that run the site: Render (hosting), Resend (sending email), OpenRouteService (drive times) and LocationIQ (address search).

Your choices

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To see or delete what we hold about you, reply to any of our emails or write to the address below.
  • California residents have these rights under the CCPA: to know, to delete, to correct, and to opt out of sale (we don't sell).

Fair housing

Brickline describes places, not people. We never rank or recommend areas by who lives there. Demographic figures are shown as published by the Census, and only if you choose to look at them.
