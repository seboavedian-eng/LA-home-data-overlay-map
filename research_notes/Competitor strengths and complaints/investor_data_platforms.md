# Investor data & valuation platforms: PropStream, PropertyRadar, HouseCanary/ComeHome. What users praise and what they complain about

Research date: 2026-11-03.

**How this was gathered (read first).** Direct page fetches were blocked in this sandbox: `www.trustpilot.com`, `www.g2.com`, `www.biggerpockets.com` and `apps.apple.com` all failed DNS ("outside the session's allowed network domains"). Every finding below therefore comes from **web-search result snippets and summaries**, not from reading the page myself. The quotes are the snippet text the search engine returned for that URL. Treat them as *probably* verbatim but unverified, and re-check them on the page before relying on one (this repo's CLAUDE.md warns that search summaries got four Glendale values wrong). The search tool also never returned a Reddit or Quora thread for any of the three products, so **Reddit sentiment is a gap**. Counts of how many users echoed a point were almost never visible in the snippets.

## What do investors, wholesalers and agents repeatedly praise about each?

### Takeaway
PropStream is praised for the breadth of its data and lists, and for its onboarding and training staff. PropertyRadar is praised by California investors for pre-foreclosure and foreclosure tracking, for owner phone and email data it has already matched (some say more accurate than PropStream's), and for responsive support. HouseCanary is praised almost entirely for AVM accuracy, but nearly all of that evidence comes from the company itself or from affiliate review sites. I found almost no hands-on investor praise for it.

### Cited Findings

- Trustpilot wholesaler snippets: the platform is "like the Bible in religion"; "PropStream is the one that works the best for me" (after trying similar tools). — [Trustpilot (IE mirror)](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/propstream.com)
- 5-star Trustpilot snippet: "Exactly what I've needed." — [Trustpilot UK p.7](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/propstream.com?page=7)
- Vendor claims, not user sentiment. Map search shows "equity data, comps, estimated rent, property classification" (July 2025). An "Estimated Wholesale Value" feature is marketed for estimating an assignment price. — [BusinessWire Jul 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250721261447/en/propstream-announces-a-new-visual-experience-for-its-map-search); [BusinessWire Nov 2024](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241119404432/en)

- Trustpilot's own summary says reviewers are largely pleased with usability and the data tools, and singles out the training sessions. Snippet: "Looking at 144 reviews, reviewers overwhelmingly had a great experience with this company." — [Trustpilot US](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/propstream.com)

- **Bias flag:** a 2026 post in the BP lead-generation thread praising the tool comes from a poster who discloses they work on PropertyRadar's team. — [BP: Best lead generation tools](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/109/topics/1205572-best-lead-generation-tools-for-real-estate-investors-what-s-your-go-to)

**HouseCanary / ComeHome: accuracy and features**
- Company-reported numbers: ComeHome median absolute prediction error is 3.1%, and "98% of all transacted properties" fall within Fitch Ratings' 80% confidence threshold. Self-reported, with no independent audit found. — [HousingWire Tech100 profile](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/48597-hw-tech100-winner-housecanary/)
- Other error figures appear on review sites: 2.7% median absolute error for the AVM, about 2.0% on on-market homes, and a 1.7% median error for 12-month HPI forecasts. These conflict with each other and with the 3.1% figure, and it is not clear which is current. — [aiandrealtors](https://aiandrealtors.com/review-housecanary); [tooliverse](https://tooliverse.ai/tools/housecanary); [realtytoolscout](https://realtytoolscout.com/tools/housecanary)
- Review-site pros: users can adjust for "property condition, recent renovations, and feature upgrades"; entry price is $19/mo; API access starts at the Pro tier. These are editorial or affiliate-style sites. — [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/housecanary); [bestaipacks](https://bestaipacks.com/tool/housecanary-review); [plumlending 2026](https://plumlending.com/insights/housecanary-review-2026)
- ComeHome: an aggregator puts it at 4.7/5 from "NLP analysis of 68 combined software reviews" (aggregator estimate, not a store figure). — [JustUseApp ComeHome](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1289334446/comehome-by-housecanary/reviews)

### Inferences
- PropStream's praise centres on *people and training* as much as on data. PropertyRadar's centres on *contact data and foreclosure workflow*, with a California tilt that fits its history (its pre-foreclosure tracking is what Sacramento and SoCal investors cite).
- For HouseCanary, the "accuracy" strength rests on the company's own numbers. Hands-on investor voices are close to absent, which suggests its main users are lenders and institutions rather than small investors.

### Gaps
- No Reddit or Quora threads surfaced for any of the three, despite several searches.
- I found no hands-on investor praise for HouseCanary's investor product, and no firsthand ComeHome review text.
- Echo counts (upvotes, "helpful" counts, number of agreeing replies) were not visible in any snippet.

## What do they repeatedly complain about?

### Takeaway
PropStream: data that is stale or wrong (owners who sold years ago, misleading equity flags on recent sales), slow support, and billing or cancellation disputes logged at the BBB. PropertyRadar: charges after a trial is cancelled, cancellation only through the web app, no refunds, prices raised during a term (one report), a clunky UI, and the best features locked to the top plan. HouseCanary: weak in rural and thin markets, enterprise orientation, tight report caps on the cheap tier, and a ComeHome app that is reportedly stale or broken.

### Cited Findings

**PropStream: accuracy and data freshness**
- BiggerPockets "Inaccurate Propstream data": a lot "he owned until 2019. Sure enough Propstream still lists him as the owner which is a few years outdated." — [BP topic 1206319](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/48/topics/1206319-inaccurate-propstream-data)
- BiggerPockets: if houses show "an owner having been there for 10+ years when that owner actually sold 8 months ago, then I might market to a completely unqualified lead." — [BP: Bad Propstream Data](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/93/topics/1105783-bad-propstream-data)
- Equity estimate flaw: "There's a race track in county deeds being filed that will falsely trigger addresses to be low equity but it actually is a recent sale with a NEW mortgage". The same user says PropStream has ownership years but "no filter on age of the debt (security deed)". — [BP topic 1206319](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/48/topics/1206319-inaccurate-propstream-data)
- Users say there is no way to tell how recently a record was updated. A 2026 review says MLS data updates daily, vacancy monthly and assessor data about yearly. — [REtipster forum: PropStream Data Out of Date](https://forum.retipster.com/t/propstream-data-out-of-date/689); [realestateskills 2026](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/propstream)
- Counterpoint from the same threads: "stale data, wrong data is an industry wide problem." — [BP topic 1206319](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/48/topics/1206319-inaccurate-propstream-data)
- AlternativeTo: "drifted away from Propstream because the data has been less reliable lately"; "quite pricey for what it's worth." — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/propstream/about)
- A long-standing BP thread is titled "why PropStream Sucks". Its year and content were not visible in the snippet. — [BP topic 1032178](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/898/topics/1032178-why-propstream-sucks)
- Trustpilot reviewers report inaccurate records and missing property values. PropStream replied that it was sorry the data had not been reliable in that area. — [Trustpilot p.6](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/propstream.com?page=6)

**PropStream: billing and cancellation (BBB)**
- "Despite these repeated requests, PropStream did not cancel the account, and on July 9, 2026, they charged me $199." — [BBB complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/lake-forest/profile/real-estate/propstream-1126-1000081302/complaints)
- "PropStream suspended my entire annual subscription while continuing to hold the $972. PropStream repeatedly says they cannot help me unless I cancel the chargeback." — [BBB complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/lake-forest/profile/real-estate/propstream-1126-1000081302/complaints)
- Company reply on BBB: "The customer was NOT charged and the account has been closed." — [BBB complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/lake-forest/profile/real-estate/propstream-1126-1000081302/complaints)

**PropStream: support and management**
- Trustpilot 2-star snippet complaining that you call and cannot get anything done. Also "customer service took forever to assist", and the company is "run like a poorly managed mom and pop shop." — [Trustpilot UK p.7](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/propstream.com?page=7); [Trustpilot IE](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/propstream.com)
- Review summary: support "could be improved due to slow response times, buffering issues, or unhelpful interactions." — [Trustpilot UK p.4](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/propstream.com?page=4)

**PropertyRadar: pricing, billing and cancellation**
- Trustpilot: "Asked to cancel free trial and they did not cancel it and now charged 75$." — [Trustpilot PropertyRadar](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/propertyradar.com)
- App Store review (single, unverified): "they are gouging their prices and raising their rates mid term because of a loophole in their user agreement". — via [realestateskills 2026](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/propertyradar-review) / [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/730602196?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)
- Support: "Had to talk to 3 different reps for a simple request with all of them giving funky robotic answers." — [Trustpilot PropertyRadar](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/propertyradar.com)
- Policy: you cancel only in the web app (Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel). A third-party guide says "you cannot make a cancellation request in person, on the phone, or via in-app chat". Paid periods are non-refundable. — [PropertyRadar pricing FAQ](https://www.propertyradar.com/pricing); [realestateskills 2026](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/propertyradar-review)
- **Pricing figures conflict:** the official page shows $249/mo, or $199/mo when prepaid annually. A 2026 review says plans run $119–$599/mo before add-ons, with Solo at $119/mo including 10,000 exports and 250 phone/email credits. Trial length is given as 5 days (official FAQ) and as 3 days elsewhere. — [PropertyRadar pricing](https://www.propertyradar.com/pricing); [realestateskills 2026](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/propertyradar-review)
- A competitor-run page titled "PropertyRadar customer reviews: not worth your money" is **biased**: Foreclosures Daily is a rival foreclosure-listing site. — [foreclosuresdaily](https://foreclosuresdaily.com/propertyradar-customer-reviews/)

**PropertyRadar: UI/UX**
- "One of the most common complaints about PropertyRadar is how it is not user-friendly". The same post says some features are locked unless you pay for the most expensive plan. — [Mashvisor blog](https://www.mashvisor.com/blog/propertyradar-real-estate-investors/amp/)
- A BP user on a free trial found it clunkier than RealtyTrac and Foreclosure.com, and said it sometimes froze in the browser (older thread). — [BP: Difference between Property Radar, RealtyTrac and Foreclosure.com](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/41/topics/465603-difference-between-property-radar-realtytrac-and-foreclosurecom)
- A Capterra quote via a review site implies users should expect "a learning curve with all the data points". — [Nerdisa](https://nerdisa.com/propertyradar/)
- Accuracy "varies by region" (aggregator). — [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/crm/propertyradar)

**HouseCanary / ComeHome**
- Accuracy "can lag in rural or low-inventory markets" where comparable sales are scarce. It "performs best in high-transaction suburban and urban markets." — [tooliverse](https://tooliverse.ai/tools/housecanary); [aiandrealtors](https://aiandrealtors.com/review-housecanary)
- The Basic tier includes only 2 custom valuation reports a month. API calls and extra reports are priced per use, so "a busy month is not the sticker price"; the product is aimed at enterprise more than at individual agents. — [bestaipacks](https://bestaipacks.com/tool/housecanary-review); [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/housecanary)
- CanaryAI is "promising but not yet fully polished" for investment workflows, and better suited to market research than to underwriting a single deal. — [plumlending 2026](https://plumlending.com/insights/housecanary-review-2026); [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/housecanary)
- ComeHome user (undated, unverified): all MLS listings are gone, making it "borderline useless". — [JustUseApp ComeHome](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1289334446/comehome-by-housecanary/reviews)
- ComeHome iOS app version 4.1.2 was last updated 2022-03-24 according to AppAdvice, which points to an app that may no longer be maintained. — [AppAdvice](https://appadvice.com/app/comehome-by-housecanary/1289334446)
- (2019) When ComeHome launched, "some MLS execs don't know how their data got on the site". — [Inman 2019](https://www.inman.com/2019/01/25/housecanary-launches-portal-raising-questions-over-listing-data/)
- (2017) Inman noted that HouseCanary's "median absolute prediction error" may not be comparable with competitors' "median error rate". — [Inman 2017](https://www.inman.com/2017/01/13/home-valuation-startup-raises-33-million/)

### Inferences
- The most damaging and best-evidenced complaint about PropStream is **stale ownership and equity data**, and it bears directly on lead-list quality. The BBB billing complaints are fewer but specific, with dollar amounts and 2026 dates.
- PropertyRadar's complaints cluster around **billing friction** (trial charges, web-only cancellation, no refunds) and **usability**, not data quality.
- The equity flaw reported for PropStream (a new purchase mortgage read as low equity) is a general risk for any equity estimate built from recorded deeds and loans. It is not unique to PropStream.

### Gaps
- California-specific complaints (for example, Prop 13 assessed value being mistaken for market value in equity estimates) were not found for any product.
- No measured skip-trace hit rates from users for any of the three.
- I could not read the "why PropStream Sucks" thread body or its date.

## Sources that proved most useful and reliable for this kind of research

- **BiggerPockets forum threads were the most useful.** They are hands-on, specific (exact years, county records, deed and mortgage mechanics) and include counterpoints. Watch for posters who disclose a vendor affiliation, and check thread dates, since many PropertyRadar threads date from 2012–2017.
- **BBB complaint pages** were the best source for billing and cancellation facts: dated, with dollar amounts and the company's reply. The volume is low, though, and the letter grade should be read on the page itself.
- **Trustpilot** was useful for volume and for vendor replies (PropStream answers 94% of negative reviews), but regional mirrors show different totals, and many excerpts are company replies rather than user text.
- **G2, Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice were weak** for these products: zero or very few reviews.
- **JustUseApp "NLP" scores, editorial "review" sites (tooliverse, bestaipacks, realestateskills, Nerdisa) and vendor comparison pages are low reliability.** Several are affiliate or SEO pages, and Foreclosures Daily is a direct competitor of PropertyRadar. They are useful for pricing and tier details, which should still be checked against the official pages.
- **Press releases** (BusinessWire, Nasdaq) cover only vendor feature claims.
- **Access limits in this run:** Trustpilot, G2, BiggerPockets and apps.apple.com could not be fetched directly (host not allowed in this sandbox), and no Reddit threads surfaced through search. A rerun with those domains allowed should verify the quotes above and add Reddit.
