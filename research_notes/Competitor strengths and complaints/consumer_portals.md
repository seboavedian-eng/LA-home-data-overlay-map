# Consumer portals: Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com — what users praise and complain about (researched 2026-11-03)

**How this was gathered, and its limits (read first):**
- **I could not read Reddit at all.** The search tool rejects `reddit.com` ("domains are not accessible to our user agent"), so there are **no Reddit verbatims** here. Some Reddit sentiment shows up second-hand in press coverage, labelled as such.
- **WebFetch could not reach any outside host** from this session (Trustpilot failed with `ENOTFOUND`, which means the host isn't on the session's allowed-network list). Every finding below comes from **search-result snippets and the search engine's summaries of each page**, not from full pages I read myself. "Quoted" text is the snippet wording; check it against the live page before publishing it as a verbatim quote.
- **No per-point echo counts** (upvotes, "X people found this helpful") were visible in the snippets, apart from the Unstar app-review tallies and the Trustpilot/BBB totals.
- App-store and review-site numbers are snapshots from different dates. Each one is labelled with what I could see.

## 1. What buyers and investors repeatedly praise about each

### Takeaway
- **Zillow** is praised for breadth: the most inventory and the default first stop.
- **Redfin** is praised for its map: draw-your-own-area search, auto-refresh as you pan, and satellite view. It also gets credit for data and research tools (estimate, First Street climate risk, GreatSchools detail).
- **Realtor.com** is praised for listing-status accuracy (because it is fed by the MLS) and for detailed filters.
- Most sources say serious buyers use two or three portals side by side.

### Cited findings

**Zillow — praise**
- Called the "default starting point for most buyers", strongest on inventory breadth. — [listwithclever (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/best-home-buying-websites/); [joefrankrealtor (2026)](https://www.joefrankrealtor.com/post/best-home-search-websites)
- A common pairing is Zillow for the main saved search plus Realtor.com for noise and commute research. — [joefrankrealtor (2026)](https://www.joefrankrealtor.com/post/best-home-search-websites)
- Google Play reviewers praise the app's "functionality in finding homes" and "user-friendly interface". This is a third-party analysis of only 13 recent reviews, so the sample is tiny. — [Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-zillow-app-review-analysis-report-google-play-tr-147401)
- App Store: 4.8★ from about 7.2M ratings (2026). — [Unstar (Jul 2026)](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-app-problems-removed-features-listing-data-2026); [apppricinglab](https://apppricinglab.com/compare/apple/284815942/310738695)

**Redfin — praise**
- The draw-your-area tool is described as "the category's most-praised feature when it works". — [Unstar home-search apps ranked (2026)](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-redfin-realtor-trulia-home-search-apps-ranked-2026)
- Listings refresh automatically as you pan or zoom, with no search button to press. — [raw.studio UX analysis](https://raw.studio/blog/using-maps-as-the-core-ux-in-real-estate-platforms)
- "Particularly strong for buyers who prefer to search visually … draw custom search boundaries on a map." — [joefrankrealtor (2026)](https://www.joefrankrealtor.com/post/best-home-search-websites)
- One reviewer: "the info on Redfin is updated more often and frequently then other similar real estate online websites." This is a single anecdote. — [Capterra Redfin reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/210572/Redfin/reviews/)
- Shows First Street climate risk (5 hazards) and GreatSchools ratings with parent reviews. Redfin kept the climate scores after Zillow dropped them. — [joefrankrealtor (2026)](https://www.joefrankrealtor.com/post/best-home-search-websites); [TechCrunch (Dec 1 2025)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/01/zillow-drops-climate-risk-scores-after-agents-complained-of-lost-sales)
- Credited with buyer rebates/fee savings and, in some sources, "the most accurate data with direct MLS access". Other sources favour Realtor.com on accuracy, so this conflicts. — [listwithclever (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/best-home-buying-websites/)
- Google 4★ (78 reviews) and Yelp 3.4★ (about 1,100 reviews) for the brokerage, mostly praising individual agents. — [listwithclever Redfin reviews (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/reviews/redfin/)

**Realtor.com — praise**
- "More accurate for listing status", while Zillow leads on breadth. — [listwithclever (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/best-home-buying-websites/)
- "One of the strongest sets of detailed filters among the major home-search websites." — [listwithclever (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/best-home-buying-websites/)
- Trustpilot's summary credits it with "nice organization of information about listings and good MLS representation". — [Trustpilot realtor.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/realtor.com)
- Ranked as having the most accurate listing data of the major apps. The same source also lists stale-listing complaints. — [Unstar ranked (2026)](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-redfin-realtor-trulia-home-search-apps-ranked-2026)
- Kept showing climate-risk data after Zillow removed it. — [TechCrunch (Dec 2025)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/01/zillow-drops-climate-risk-scores-after-agents-complained-of-lost-sales)

**Investors (BiggerPockets)**
- Investors use Zillow, Redfin, Rentometer and BiggerPockets' own estimator as **screening tools**, then check against local rental comps or ask a leasing agent. A Southern California landlord got three different rent figures for one 5-bed house. — [BP: Rent estimate accuracy Zillow vs Redfin vs Rentometer](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/52/topics/1206540-rent-estimate-accuracy-of-zillow-vs-redfin-vs-rent-o-meter); [BP: Most accurate rent estimator](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/921/topics/1153346-most-accurate-rent-estimator)

### Inferences
- The praise splits by strength: Zillow for breadth, Redfin for map and data, Realtor.com for status accuracy. That fits the "use two or three apps" advice.
- Almost all of the praise is about search and discovery. Very little is about due diligence on a specific lot (zoning, permits, lot room).

### Gaps
- No Reddit or Quora verbatims, because both were unreachable. No upvote counts.
- No direct source on how fresh each site's sold data is, and nothing on California's sold-price disclosure specifically.

## 2. What they repeatedly complain about

### Takeaway
- **Zillow:** the Zestimate's reliability off-market, stale "active" listings, the Dec 2025 removal of climate scores, contact-agent routing to paid Premier Agent/Flex agents (now the subject of a class action), and 2026 app regressions.
- **Redfin:** an app that pushes Redfin's own agents, "recommended" sorting that steers you, unresponsive brokerage agents (BBB F), and Rocket Mortgage cross-selling since the July 2025 acquisition.
- **Realtor.com:** the heaviest agent lead spam, stale listings, and bugs after a redesign.

### Cited findings

**Zillow — accuracy and estimates**
- Zillow's own figures: median Zestimate error is 1.9% for on-market homes and 7.49% (about 7.5%) for off-market homes. Some sites cite 2.4% on-market, so the numbers aren't consistent. — [houwzer (2026)](https://houwzer.com/blog/the-zillow-zestimate-can-it-be-trusted/)
- LA: a **2019** Duvora study (3 sample homes per city, too small to rely on) found LA Zestimates about 5% above sale prices. — [Matthew Hoult Estates blog](https://www.matthewhoult.com/blog/how-accurate-is-your-zestimate)
- LA: an agent blog says Palisades Zestimates showed pre-fire values for months after the January 2025 fire. **Unverified; this is the only source.** — [ownluxuryhomes (2026)](https://www.ownluxuryhomes.com/markets/national/zillow-ai-accuracy/how-accurate-is-zillow-zestimate-2026)
- Its roughly 30-minute refresh means "you'll occasionally see homes listed as active when they're already under contract". — [listwithclever (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/best-home-buying-websites/)
- BiggerPockets view: Zillow rent estimates "tend to run high" because they lean on listed rents. The tools miss unit condition and micro-neighbourhood trends. — [BP thread](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/52/topics/1206540-rent-estimate-accuracy-of-zillow-vs-redfin-vs-rent-o-meter)

**Zillow — climate risk removal (late Nov / Dec 2025)**
- First Street climate scores were pulled from more than 1M listings after agents complained of lost sales, following objections from the **California Regional MLS (CRMLS)** about the accuracy of the flood models. They were replaced with a less visible link to First Street. Redfin, Realtor.com and Homes.com still show them. — [TechCrunch (Dec 1 2025)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/01/zillow-drops-climate-risk-scores-after-agents-complained-of-lost-sales); [RISMedia (Dec 2 2025)](https://www.rismedia.com/2025/12/02/zillow-removes-climate-risk-scores/)
- California Insurance Commissioner Ricardo Lara: buyers "make the biggest financial decision of their lives while flying blind". Press also reports a Reddit user saying they now had to go back to county assessor sites for flood data (second-hand). — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/zillow-sparks-outrage-removing-critical-115000050.html)
- A California climate expert is working to restore the scores. — [Times of San Diego (Jan 26 2026)](https://timesofsandiego.com/tech/2026/01/26/zillow-deleted-its-climate-risk-scores-a-california-climate-expert-is-working-to-restore-them/); [Inside Climate News](https://insideclimatenews.org/?p=104818)

**Zillow — lead-gen and agent contact**
- The class action (*Taylor v. Zillow*, W.D. Wash. 2:25-cv-01818) alleges that "Contact Agent" and "Request a Tour" send buyers to paid Zillow Flex agents rather than the listing agent, and that buyers must sign a "Touring Agreement". It also alleges Flex agents pay Zillow 40% of their commission and are pressured to steer buyers to Zillow Home Loans. RICO claims were added in Nov 2025 and the cases were merged in Dec 2025. **These are allegations.** One report says a judge threw out the central claims of a deception suit (ruling date not confirmed). — [Amended complaint PDF (Dec 29 2025)](https://www.cohenmilstein.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2025.12.29-Taylor-v.-Zillow-Consolidated-Amended-Complaint.pdf); [Real Estate News (Nov 19 2025)](https://www.realestatenews.com/2025/11/19/zillow-hit-with-expanded-class-action-adding-rico-claims); [Real Estate News (Dec 11 2025)](https://www.realestatenews.com/2025/12/11/court-merges-2-class-action-cases-against-zillow); [ArcaMax (judge tosses suit)](https://www.arcamax.com/homeandleisure/consumer/home/s-4265087)
- A consumer guide warns that a tour request can bring "repeated follow-up calls or texts" and a request to sign an agreement before you enter the home. — [robbieenglish.com](https://www.robbieenglish.com/blog/the-hidden-risks-of-zillows-request-a-tour-button-what-you-should-know/)

**Zillow — app (2026)**
- Unstar analysed 380 one-to-three-star reviews (300 from Google Play US plus the US App Store feed, pulled 31 Jul 2026). Negative reviews rose each month: Mar 38 → Apr 66 → May 68 → Jun 99 → Jul 109. Top themes: removed features and listing data, filters (37 mentions), and a June 2026 update that "completely broke the Commute/Travel Time feature". The report button opens "a nonspecific help database with no recourse". Unstar sells review analysis, so its framing may be promotional. — [Unstar (2026)](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-app-problems-removed-features-listing-data-2026)
- Trustpilot gives Zillow 1.5/5 from 547 reviews on the UK domain. Many complaints there are about rentals and landlord tools, and the summary notes "significant problems with customer service". — [Trustpilot UK](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/zillow.com)

**Redfin**
- "Pushy" about its own agents for users who don't want one. The default "recommended" sort "will steer you toward Redfin properties". — [Capterra Redfin reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/210572/Redfin/reviews/)
- The draw tool is also the "most-complained-about feature when it doesn't [work]". Users report map pin errors and the draw tool snapping to the wrong boundary on iOS. — [Unstar ranked (2026)](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-redfin-realtor-trulia-home-search-apps-ranked-2026)
- Brokerage complaints are mostly about communication: unresponsive agents, ignored messages, missed appointments. — [listwithclever Redfin reviews (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/reviews/redfin/)
- Weaker for rentals, because it focuses on sales. — [rentalrealestate.com](https://rentalrealestate.com/tools/redfin/)
- Only works in areas with Redfin agents. — [listwithclever (2026)](https://listwithclever.com/best-home-buying-websites/)
- Its GreatSchools ratings have lagged GreatSchools' own site (**2015**, Chicago). — [Inman (2015)](https://www.inman.com/2015/07/01/redfins-sometimes-outdated-greatschools-ratings-raise-concerns/); [Crain's Chicago (2015)](https://www.chicagobusiness.com/article/20150630/CRED0701/150629793/redfin-school-ratings-wrong-on-many-chicago-listings)

**Realtor.com**
- Agent spam is "the highest in its category". Users "frequently complain about being contacted by multiple agents immediately after property inquiries". — [Unstar ranked (2026)](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-redfin-realtor-trulia-home-search-apps-ranked-2026); [plumlending](https://plumlending.com/insights/realtor-com-review)
- Stale listings: rentals stay active after the units are gone, and some homes shown are already sold. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/realtor.com); [Unstar ranked](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-redfin-realtor-trulia-home-search-apps-ranked-2026)
- After a redesign: "buggy for weeks now", "maps are bad", and a search for waterfront homes "shows me acreage". — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/realtor.com)
- Many Trustpilot/BBB complaints come from **agents** (the Listing Toolkit and lead quality), not home buyers. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/realtor.com?page=2)

**School data (all three)**
- GreatSchools ratings are criticised for tracking demographics more than school quality. A Change.org petition asks Zillow, Realtor.com and Redfin to drop them. Zillow removed the colour coding in **2016**. In Baltimore, Zillow acknowledged a problem with how GreatSchools data is attached to listings. — [Chalkbeat (2019)](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2019/12/5/21121858/looking-for-a-home-you-ve-seen-greatschools-ratings-here-s-how-they-nudge-families-toward-schools-wi/); [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/zillow-remove-greatschools-rankings-from-zillow-and-realtor-com); [Inman (2016)](https://www.inman.com/2016/12/20/zillow-wipes-colors-school-ratings-media-coverage/); [The Banner](https://www.thebanner.com/economy/real-estate/zillow-baltimore-city-schools-YPEXAAKHYRDGDBFFZCZSOLYPFQ/)

### Inferences
- Lead-gen pressure is the complaint shared by all three. It comes from the business model: the portals make money by routing buyers to agents or lenders.
- For an LA buyer, the climate-score removal matters most. It was triggered by a California MLS, and LA buyers face wildfire and flood exposure.

### Gaps
- I found no evidence that GreatSchools was removed outright from any of the three in 2024–2026.
- No LA-specific Reddit complaints (Reddit was unreachable).

## 4. Notable changes in 2024–2026 that users reacted to

### Cited findings
- **Zillow drops First Street climate scores** (late Nov 2025), after CRMLS objected. Criticised by consumer advocates and the California Insurance Commissioner. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/01/zillow-drops-climate-risk-scores-after-agents-complained-of-lost-sales); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/zillow-sparks-outrage-removing-critical-115000050.html)
- **Zillow Listing Access Standards** (enforced from June 30 2025). Zillow bans listings marketed publicly but not put on the MLS within one business day. A judge denied Compass's request to block the rule on Feb 6 2026. "Zillow Preview" for pre-market homes launched in Mar 2026. — [Yahoo Finance summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/zillow-sparks-outrage-removing-critical-115000050.html); [effectiveagents](https://www.effectiveagents.com/resources/zillow-preview-explained-how-listing-agents-now-earn-10)
- **Zillow app regressions in 2026:** negative reviews rose from March to July 2026, and a June update broke the commute feature. — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/zillow-app-problems-removed-features-listing-data-2026)
- **Zillow lead-routing class action** (2025, RICO claims added Nov 2025, cases merged Dec 2025). — [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2025/11/19/zillow-hit-with-expanded-class-action-adding-rico-claims)
- **Rocket Companies buys Redfin** (closed in 2025). The app now says "Redfin is now part of Rocket Companies" and has a "Get prequalified" button that goes to Rocket Mortgage. Third-party lender placements were replaced by Rocket. Rocket Homes search was shut down and sent to Redfin. Rocket laid off about 2% of staff for overlapping roles. A $6,000 buyer credit is offered. — [The Truth About Mortgage](https://www.thetruthaboutmortgage.com/rocket-mortgage-completes-redfin-takeover-offers-6000-home-buyer-credit/); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/rocket-revamps-real-estate-arm-moves-home-search-to-redfin/); [Real Estate News (Jul 31 2025)](https://www.realestatenews.com/2025/07/31/redfin-already-funneling-customers-to-rocket-ceo-says)
- **Realtor.com redesign** brought bug and map complaints (exact date not visible). — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/realtor.com)

### Inferences
- Since 2024, users have mainly reacted to features being **taken away or monetised**: climate scores, lender choice, and contact-agent routing. They have not reacted much to new features.

### Gaps
- I found no consumer reaction to AI or natural-language search on any of the three.
- No dated reaction to a Realtor.com product change in 2024–2026 beyond the redesign complaints.

## Most useful and reliable sources for this kind of research

- **Most reliable for facts:** TechCrunch, RISMedia, Real Estate News and HousingWire, together with court filings and BBB profile pages. They give dated, checkable facts, such as the climate-score removal, the lawsuit, the Rocket deal and BBB grades.
- **Most useful for sentiment themes:** Unstar's app-review analyses. They give counts and dated windows, but the company sells review analysis, so treat its framing with care. Trustpilot and BBB summaries work as complaint inventories, but they over-represent agents, renters and brokerage clients.
- **Useful for investors:** BiggerPockets threads. They are anecdotal but practical, and tell you what investors actually do with each tool.
- **Weak:** comparison blogs from brokerages and lead-gen firms (listwithclever, houzeo, agent blogs). They have a commercial interest and contradict each other on which site is "most accurate".
- **Unreachable here:** Reddit (blocked from search) and every live page by fetch (network allow-list). A second pass with `trustpilot.com`, `apps.apple.com`, `play.google.com`, `bbb.org` and `biggerpockets.com` allowed in the session's network settings would be needed to collect true verbatims and current counts.
