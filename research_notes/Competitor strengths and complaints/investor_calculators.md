# Investor calculators: what users praise and complain about in DealCheck, Mashvisor and BiggerPockets Pro

Research date: 2026-11-03.

**How this was gathered.** Direct page fetches failed with a DNS/allowlist error for every review host tried: trustpilot.com (and au.), biggerpockets.com, capterra.com, apps.apple.com and onemoredoor.com. That means **every quote and rating below comes from web-search result snippets or search-engine summaries**. None was read on the page itself. Treat the quotes as near-verbatim and check them against the URL before publishing. On the hosts that failed, "how many users echoed this" (upvotes, helpful counts) was never visible, so most counts are missing. To verify the figures, these hosts must be allowed in the session's network settings.

## What small investors repeatedly praise about each tool

### Takeaway
- **DealCheck:** speed, breadth of strategies (rental, BRRRR, flip, multifamily) and a usable free tier/mobile app.
- **Mashvisor:** market and neighborhood screening (heatmaps, long-term and Airbnb side by side), plus generally responsive support.
- **BiggerPockets Pro:** the calculators (flip/BRRRR/rental) bundled with community and perks, not the data.

### Cited Findings

**DealCheck: features and UX**
- Switching from BP Pro: "Coming from a pricey BiggerPockets Pro membership, this app hits the nail right on the head. DealCheck provides more analytical tools and metrics for analyzing complex deals." (Trustpilot, date not visible) — [Trustpilot DealCheck](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/dealcheck.io)
- A Capterra reviewer praised how quickly you can adjust an investment and see the return. — [Capterra DealCheck](https://www.capterra.com/p/205089/DealCheck/reviews/)
- On comps: "an awesome tool for rent comps and sales comps". Another reviewer called the ARV, rehab and rent figures accurate and said the tool saves switching between apps. — [Trustpilot DealCheck](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/dealcheck.io)
- App Store users describe it as "like a fancy excel spreadsheet Wizard" (positive, with a caveat below). They also call the comps feature useful for seeing recent nearby sales and rentals. — [App Store DealCheck reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1001869134?see-all=reviews)
- Vendor's own listing: a full analysis covering "closing costs, mortgage payment, cash flow, ROI, profit and more" for rental, BRRRR, flip, rehab, multifamily and commercial deals; claims 350,000+ users (vendor marketing). — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dealcheck-analyze-real-estate/id1001869134)

**Mashvisor: features, support, screening value**
- Trustpilot praise centres on support and ease of use. Users call it "accommodating and responsive" when they cancelled for personal reasons. A 2026 reviewer says the team was "responsive and helpful" during onboarding and with technical questions. — [Trustpilot Mashvisor (SE)](https://se.trustpilot.com/review/www.mashvisor.com); [Trustpilot Mashvisor (AU)](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.mashvisor.com)
- Support refunded users who missed the trial-cancel window by a day or two. One user: they "provided a refund after the trial ended before I was able to cancel in time." — [Trustpilot Mashvisor (IE p6)](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.mashvisor.com?page=6)
- G2 (one review, 2021): praised batch property analysis. — [G2 Mashvisor](https://www.g2.com/products/mashvisor/reviews)
- Hands-on 2026 review (Real Estate Skills, editorial; it sells courses, check for affiliate links). Strengths: long-term and Airbnb analysis side by side, heatmap screening, STR regulation data for 500+ cities. Verdict: "accurate enough to tell you where to look". — [Real Estate Skills Mashvisor review](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/mashvisor-review)
- 10xbnb (2026, STR-operator blog; may carry affiliate links) says it is worth $49.99–$99.99/mo for early-stage market comparison and quick cap-rate and cash-on-cash math. — [10xbnb](https://learn.10xbnb.com/mashvisor-review/)
- An older BP forum user (about 2018–19) found it "fairly accurate in the Smokies". — [BP forum: Any else use Mashvisor?](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/725/topics/715383-any-else-use-mashvisor-how-accurate-is-it)

**BiggerPockets Pro: calculators, community, perks**
- Forum members cite the "flip, BRRRR, multi... calculators which are awesome!!" as the main draw. — [BP forum: bigger pockets for what](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/55/topics/1293035-bigger-pockets-for-what)
- A member blogger who renews yearly values the badge and networking: "Paying for BiggerPockets Pro creates a strong buy-in to the community and motivates you to learn more, faster." **Note:** a search summary flagged one enthusiastic endorser in these results as a BP employee. — [BP member blog: Is BiggerPockets Pro worth your money](https://www.biggerpockets.com/member-blogs/10341/80188-is-biggerpockets-pro-worth-your-money)
- Perks claimed at "up to $5,000" in annual savings (loan fee credits, insurance discounts). This is from a marketing-adjacent review, so it is unverified. — [Ark7 BP Pro review](https://ark7.com/blog/articles/biggerpockets-pro-review/)
- The BiggerPockets mobile app (new app ID 6736392302) is rated highly; see the ratings table below. — [App Store BiggerPockets](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/biggerpockets/id6736392302)

### Inferences
- DealCheck is praised as a *calculator*: inputs, speed, report quality. Mashvisor is praised as a *screening/discovery* tool. BP Pro is praised as a *bundle*. None is consistently praised for the accuracy of its automatic numbers.

### Gaps
- Echo counts (helpful votes, thread reply counts) could not be seen because the page fetches were blocked.
- No Reddit threads surfaced in any search for these three tools. Reddit sentiment remains unknown; one claim about Reddit came second-hand from bnbcalc and is unverified.

## What they repeatedly complain about

### Takeaway
- **Mashvisor:** the loudest complaints are about accuracy (inflated occupancy and rents, thin rural data) and billing (quarterly or annual auto-renewals, no refunds after renewal, cancellations users thought had gone through).
- **DealCheck:** complaints are milder. They cover pre-filled data and comps quality, free-tier save limits, and limited line-item customisation.
- **BP Pro:** complaints are about auto-renewal and refunds, value for beginners, and a rent estimator forum users call inaccurate.

### Cited Findings

**Mashvisor: accuracy (rent, occupancy, CoC)**
- Expert-tier user: a listing showed twenty nights booked in a month when actual bookings were ten. "Mashvisor is grossly overestimating the occupancy." (about 2018–19) — [BP forum: Any else use Mashvisor? How accurate is it?](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/725/topics/715383-any-else-use-mashvisor-how-accurate-is-it)
- Same thread: traditional-rental numbers misleading, rehab underestimated or rents inflated, data appeared to come from Zillow. "When you adjust the numbers, their 'good deals' don't look so good anymore." Florida/Disney-area data showed "a slew of either very high revenues or extremely low revenues." — same URL
- Orlando user (as quoted in a 2026 review): Airbnb occupancy matched "the 75th percentile of actual listings, not the median". — [Real Estate Skills](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/mashvisor-review) citing [BP forum: Accuracy of Mashvisor data/analysis?](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/530/topics/530420-accuracy-of-mashvisor-data-analysis)
- Older thread: data "so inaccurate that it was completely unusable" (2018–2019). — [BP forum: Thoughts on Mashvisor?](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/88/topics/413555-thoughts-on-mashvisor)
- 2026 editorial: "not accurate enough to tell you what to pay". Estimates are unreliable outside major metros, and a cash-on-cash figure built on default expenses is "close to meaningless". — [Real Estate Skills](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/mashvisor-review)
- Firmtools (2026): AirDNA may be better for STR-only investors, for deeper granularity, or for smaller markets where Mashvisor's data "thins out". — [Firmtools Mashvisor](https://firmtools.ai/mashvisor/)
- Spark Rental: "some users report occasional inaccuracies in data". — [Spark Rental](https://sparkrental.com/mashvisor-review/)

**Mashvisor: coverage and data freshness**
- 2025 Trustpilot: "the portal is difficult to navigate and specific data in rural areas is not readily available. Also it is unclear how often the data is updated." — [Trustpilot Mashvisor](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.mashvisor.com?page=7)
- Mashvisor's own blog admits some users "don't like how the data is updated only monthly and not in real-time". Its marketing elsewhere claims weekly updates across 400+ metros and 10,000+ zips, so the vendor contradicts itself. — [Mashvisor blog: reviews](https://www.mashvisor.com/blog/mashvisor-reviews/) vs [Mashvisor blog: Airbnb data](https://www.mashvisor.com/blog/airbnb-data)

**Mashvisor: pricing and billing**
- Trustpilot: "I thought I cancelled it but it then billed me 3 months later" (quarterly billing). Company reply: plans must be cancelled before the renewal date, and payments after renewal are not refunded. — [Trustpilot Mashvisor](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.mashvisor.com?page=3)
- 1-star reviewer asked for a refund the day after renewal. Support first said "doesn't offer refunds for payments made after the renewal date", then: "We are happy to make an exception and refund the most recent charge." — [Trustpilot Mashvisor](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.mashvisor.com?page=6)
- Other Trustpilot complaints: unanswered support, free trials that turned into charges, alleged fake listings and inflated numbers. One user got a full refund only after threatening legal action. — [Trustpilot Mashvisor](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.mashvisor.com)
- BBB: 11 complaints in the last 3 years, 4 closed in the last 12 months (a third-party crawl showed 9/2, so the counts changed between snapshots). One Dec 2024 complaint about an **$899.88** charge after attempted cancellation is listed as unanswered. Another filer says quarterly charges continued after a bank dispute and refund. — [BBB Mashvisor complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/campbell/profile/investment-advisory-services/mashvisor-1216-651297/complaints); [ForeclosuresDaily summary (competitor)](https://foreclosuresdaily.com/competitors/mashvisor-competitor-reviews/)
- 2026 pricing cited: $49.99–$99.99/mo billed annually, "no free trial or refund". — [Real Estate Skills](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/mashvisor-review)
- A BP thread is titled "Beware Mashvisor bait and switch". The body could not be retrieved, so the specifics are unknown. — [BP forum](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/88/topics/1293786-beware-mashvisor-bait-and-switch)
- Cancel path per the help centre: Billing tab → "Cancel Plan"; otherwise chat or email. — [Mashvisor help](https://help.mashvisor.com/en/articles/1303556-how-do-i-cancel-my-subscription)

**Mashvisor: UI/UX**
- G2 (2021): getting information out of the platform was harder than it should be, and usability could be better. — [G2 Mashvisor](https://www.g2.com/products/mashvisor/reviews)

**DealCheck: accuracy, comps and limits**
- App Store: comps "returning comps that are not actually solds" is a data problem. — [App Store DealCheck reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1001869134?see-all=reviews)
- A competitor review says complaints cluster on the accuracy of pre-filled data and comps. **Competitor source.** — [OneMoreDoor](https://onemoredoor.com/learn/dealcheck-review)
- Free-tier limit: "just delete a few properties when I hit the limit of (14?)". — [App Store DealCheck reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1001869134?see-all=reviews)
- Users still have to find insurance, rehab and tax figures themselves ("fancy excel spreadsheet Wizard"). — same URL
- Capterra con: not every line item or detail is customisable. — [Capterra DealCheck](https://www.capterra.com/p/205089/DealCheck/reviews/)

**DealCheck: pricing, billing and support**
- Plans: Plus about $10/mo with capped saves, comps and templates (about 50 properties per the competitor page). Pro $29/mo or $20/mo billed annually, with unlimited properties, comps, templates and reports, and a 14-day trial on paid tiers. — [DealRun pricing breakdown (competitor blog, Aug 2026)](https://dealrun.ai/blog/dealcheck-pricing-breakdown); [Real Estate Skills DealCheck](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/dealcheck-review)
- Refunds conflict. An older guide (about 2022) says "does not offer any refunds once charged" and that cancelling drops you to free at the next billing date. A newer one (about 2025) mentions a money-back guarantee. Neither is official. — [RealEstateBees](https://realestatebees.com/software/dealcheck/); [RentalRecon](https://www.rentalrecon.com/property-management-tools/dealcheck-review/)
- Support: mostly praised, but individual reviews include "Poor customer service". Live chat runs during business hours; email is support@dealcheck.io. — [Software Advice DealCheck reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/investment-management/dealcheck-profile/reviews/)

**BiggerPockets Pro: billing, auto-renewal and refunds**
- BBB complaint (Oct 2025): charged a renewal after requesting cancellation; asked for a refund and written confirmation. — [BBB BiggerPockets complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/co/denver/profile/real-estate-services/biggerpockets-inc-1296-90142685/complaints)
- BP's stated policy (company replies): auto-renewal is disclosed, with a reminder 45 days before. After renewal, refunds are prorated ("refund the difference"). The refund team works "Mon-Friday, 9-4pm MST". — same BBB URL
- Reviews.io: partial refunds only, even on same-day cancellation requests. — [Reviews.io BiggerPockets](https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/biggerpockets)
- Forum: unanswered refund requests, cancellations taking effect later than requested, and one user had their bank stop payment (several threads are older). — [BP forum: Trying to cancel BP pro membership](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/25/topics/1121397-trying-to-cancel-bp-pro-membership); [Cancelling My BP Membership](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/25/topics/892664-cancelling-my-bigger-pockets-membership)
- Cancel path disputed: guides say self-serve in settings; Firmtools says email support. — [Firmtools BP Pro](https://firmtools.ai/biggerpockets-pro/)
- Price cited (2026): $39/mo monthly or $390/yr. — [bnbcalc BP review 2026 (competitor/affiliate)](https://www.bnbcalc.com/pt/reviews/biggerpockets-review-2026)

**BiggerPockets Pro: value for money**
- "There are nice features associated with the pro membership, but they're most likely not all that valuable to you if you are completely new to real estate." — [BP member blog](https://www.biggerpockets.com/member-blogs/10341/80188-is-biggerpockets-pro-worth-your-money)
- A comparison blog says the BP calculator lacks depth for lender-approval or vacancy-sensitivity analysis, and that free use is capped. **Shopfolio sells a competing spreadsheet.** — [Shopfolio](https://shopfolio.store/blog/best-real-estate-deal-analyzer-spreadsheet)
- Forum quality varies, "expert-level to anecdotal". — [Ark7](https://ark7.com/blog/articles/biggerpockets-pro-review/)

**BiggerPockets: Rent Estimator accuracy (forum threads, about 2022–2025)**
- Titles alone signal the problem: "What's a point to use Rent Estimator if it's so far from truth". — [BP forum](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/853/topics/1173849-whats-a-point-to-use-rent-estimator-if-its-so-far-from-truth)
- A $1,500 rental showed $650; the tool "usually shows lower numbers compared to Rentometer". — [BP forum: Rentometer and BP Rent Estimator](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/52/topics/984857-rentometer-and-bp-rent-estimator)
- "Horribly inaccurate for my market" (Cape Cod agent); "always inaccurate". — [BP forum: Most Accurate Rent Estimator](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/921/topics/1153346-most-accurate-rent-estimator); [Using BP Rent Estimator Tool](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/432/topics/1143190-using-biggerpockets-rent-estimator-tool)
- Multifamily: appears to use single-family rates and doesn't break out per unit. — [BP forum: Rental Estimates for Multi-Family](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/432/topics/1145013-rental-estimates-for-multi-family)
- Consensus advice: cross-check against Rentometer, Zillow and active listings; users want transparent comps behind the number. — same threads

**BiggerPockets: mobile**
- A recent App Store review complains that a redesign removed keyword notifications. — [App Store BiggerPockets reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/biggerpockets/id6736392302?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)

### Inferences
- **A pattern across all three:** automated rent and occupancy numbers lose user trust whenever the comps behind them are hidden. Users repeatedly ask to see the comps, or override them and cross-check against Rentometer, Zillow or AirDNA.
- **Billing is the biggest reputational risk:** Mashvisor and BP Pro both draw complaints when an auto-renewal (quarterly or annual) is followed by a strict refund policy. DealCheck has far fewer billing complaints in what surfaced.

### Gaps
- No California/LA-specific sentiment found for any of the three tools.
- The body of the "bait and switch" thread could not be read.
- No Reddit primary threads found.
- DealCheck's official refund terms were not found.

## Which sources proved most useful and reliable for this kind of research

These are working notes for the report writer, kept inside the required three-section structure.

- **BiggerPockets forum threads** were the most specific source on *accuracy*: concrete numbers ($1,500 vs $650; 20 vs 10 nights), named markets and practical workarounds. The drawbacks: many Mashvisor threads are 2013–2019, and BP has an obvious conflict of interest when it discusses its own Pro product.
- **BBB complaint pages** were the best source on *billing*: dated, with dollar amounts and the company's replies, which state policy (BP's 45-day reminder and prorated refunds). The sample is small.
- **Trustpilot** gave large samples (Mashvisor 482, BP 204) plus company replies that reveal refund policy. It skews toward extremes, and its regional mirrors show the same reviews.
- **G2/Capterra** have too few reviews for these tools to help much (Mashvisor: 1 review on G2).
- **Review blogs** are mostly competitor or affiliate content: Mashvisor reviewing DealCheck, OneMoreDoor, DealRun, bnbcalc, Airbtics, Shopfolio. Real Estate Skills and 10xbnb show some hands-on testing but may carry affiliate links.
- **Reddit** surfaced nothing via search in this session.
- **Fetch access:** allowing trustpilot.com, biggerpockets.com, capterra.com, apps.apple.com and play.google.com would allow verbatim checks and echo counts.
